Schools November 2026

5 signs your school needs a fees management system

If the bursar is still adding up receipts by hand at the end of every term, your school is losing time and probably money too.

  • Balances don't agree. Receipts, bank slips and the ledger give three different totals.
  • Parents wait for answers. Nobody can tell a parent their child's balance on the spot.
  • Reports take days. Term reports for the board are put together by hand.
  • Defaulters are missed. Students with balances aren't followed up in time.
  • Records live in one book. If it gets lost or damaged, the history goes with it.

A fees system records every payment once, prints receipts and gives you balances and reports instantly.

Websites October 2026

Why every small business in Kampala needs a website

Most customers search online before they buy. Without a website, they find your competitors first.

  • Be found. Show up when people search for your service on Google.
  • Look trustworthy. A professional site makes a small business look established.
  • Open 24/7. Customers can see your prices, location and contacts any time.
  • Get more enquiries. WhatsApp, call and contact buttons turn visitors into customers.

A mobile-friendly website costs less than you might think, and we can have yours online quickly.

Printing September 2026

How to get ID cards that last the whole year

Cheap ID cards fade, crack and peel within weeks. Here's what makes the difference.

  • Use PVC cards, not laminated paper. They don't bend or peel.
  • Send clear photos taken against a plain background.
  • Check names before printing. Send us your list in Excel to avoid typing mistakes.
  • Add a barcode or QR code for attendance, library and gate checks.

We design and print IDs for schools, companies and groups. Order early for the start of term.
